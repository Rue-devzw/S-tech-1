# Customer Portal Guide

The OmniTech customer portal is designed for non-technical users who need repairs, installations, ICT support, websites, software or automation help.

## Create an Account

1. Go to `/customer/register`.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number and organisation if applicable.
3. Choose a strong password.
4. Submit the form.
5. You will be signed in and can open `/customer`.

## Sign In

1. Go to `/login`.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. After login, customer users are sent to the customer portal.

## Request a Service

1. Open the portal.
2. Choose the service request option.
3. Select the type of service:
   - Phone, PC, laptop, TV or electronics repair.
   - ICT support.
   - Networking.
   - Starlink-related installation support.
   - Web development.
   - Software, AI or automation.
4. Describe the issue or goal in plain language.
5. Add location and preferred date where relevant.
6. Upload or select device/site photos where available.
7. Submit the request.

Useful details to include:

- Device make and model.
- What happened before the fault started.
- Error messages.
- Whether the fault is constant or intermittent.
- Site address for field work.
- Urgency and preferred contact method.

## Track Repair or Job Status

The portal shows service history and active work. Statuses may include:

- Request received.
- Job created.
- Diagnosis in progress.
- Quotation sent.
- Awaiting approval.
- Waiting for parts.
- Repair/installation in progress.
- Testing.
- Ready for collection.
- Invoice issued.
- Payment received.
- Completed.
- Warranty active.

For public status checks, use `/track-repair` when you have a tracking reference.

## Quotations

When OmniTech sends a quotation:

1. Open the quotation in the portal.
2. Review line items, labour, parts, discounts, tax-ready fields and total.
3. Check expiry date and notes.
4. Approve or reject the quotation.

Work that requires payment approval should not continue until you approve the quotation.

## Invoices and Receipts

Customers can view invoices and receipts in the portal.

- Download or open invoices when issued.
- Check payment status.
- Keep receipts for warranty and accounting records.

## Book Installations

For field services, provide:

- Site address.
- Contact person on site.
- Phone number.
- Preferred date/time.
- Access notes.
- Photos of roof/site/equipment where relevant.

For Starlink-related services, OmniTech provides independent installation, setup and support services and does not claim official Starlink partnership unless explicitly stated by the business later.

## Messages

Use portal messages or listed contact channels to:

- Add missing details.
- Ask for updates.
- Respond to quotation questions.
- Request warranty support.

Keep messages focused on the job reference where possible.

## Warranty Claims

To submit warranty support:

1. Open the completed job or warranty record.
2. Describe the issue.
3. Add photos or evidence where helpful.
4. Submit the claim.

Warranty review depends on the original warranty terms, fault type and evidence.

## Ratings and Feedback

After completion, rate the work and leave feedback. This helps OmniTech improve repair quality, communication, installation handover and follow-up support.

## Privacy

OmniTech uses customer data for service delivery, communication, billing and after-service support.

Do not submit passwords unless explicitly requested through a secure process. If a technician needs device access, ask how the access details will be protected.

## Getting Help

If you cannot sign in or cannot find a job:

- Confirm you are using the same phone/email used for the request.
- Check your tracking reference.
- Contact OmniTech with your name, phone number and service type.
